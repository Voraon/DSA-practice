In micro frontend there was a race condition to get business .

Due to race condition sometime the business api call was success full, sometime it was failing due to
verfication api was still pending. To resolve this, had to make the operation synchrenous understanding the business 
requirement. As it's critical point . Other than make this synchrenous , the api will eventually fail.
Hence make a promise chain , on the successful validation of jwt token, then make the business selection api call
